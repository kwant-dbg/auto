# 09 — Answers to the 18 Open Architectural Questions (v1.0 §22)

Short answers with pointers to the detailed rationale.

| # | Question | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Minimum viable discovery pipeline?** | 3–4 spend-revealing sources → extractor with verbatim-span verification → normalise + embed → cluster into problems → triage gates → question-driven research on top few → weekly digest of ≤ 3 cards + founder feedback + baseline comparison. Preceded by a 1-week manual Wizard-of-Oz. (`06` §2–3) |
| 2 | **Temporal vs. lightweight scheduler?** | Postgres `SKIP LOCKED` queue + cron for MVP–Phase 3. Adopt Temporal when ≥ 3 workflow types have multi-day human-signal waits or you start hand-writing compensation logic. Keep tasks idempotent so the switch is cheap. (`04` §6) |
| 3 | **Separate workers or dynamic roles?** | Dynamic roles: one worker pool; task type → (prompt, tools, schema, model, budget). ~4 LLM roles + red team + later builder; orchestration in code. (`04` §1) |
| 4 | **Controlling autonomous task creation?** | Agents *propose* (≤ 3 per task, depth ≤ 2); a deterministic admission controller *admits* only tasks linked to a question and factor, passing dedupe and within the opportunity's earned budget. (`04` §5) |
| 5 | **Deduplicating problems across unrelated sources?** | Normalise each observation to `actor | job | obstacle | consequence` *before* embedding; ANN attach above a high threshold, LLM adjudication in the ambiguous band (same / parent-child / sibling / different); hierarchical problems; weekly density re-cluster; thresholds calibrated on ~200 labelled pairs. (`02` §6) |
| 6 | **Calibrating commercial scores?** | Don't score; estimate ranged factors and compute EV distributions in code. Calibrate in stages: founder agreement (weeks) → Brier scores on pre-registered experiment predictions (months) with optimism shrinkage → realised MRR vs. P10/P50/P90 (sanity only). (`03` §9) |
| 7 | **Objectively evaluating research quality?** | Mechanical span validity (100%), entailment pass rate, founder spot-check precision, competitor recall on gold sets, inter-run consistency, known-answer probes. (`07` §2–3) |
| 8 | **Best sources by signal/cost?** | In order: O*NET × BLS × AI-usage indices (free, structured, offline-industry coverage); app marketplaces (spend + distribution); public procurement (actual payments); paid templates & productised-service price lists (purchase evidence, sampled); pay-transparent job postings; change-event feeds. Complaint channels for *corroboration*. (`02` §2) |
| 9 | **Modelling freshness, independence, reliability?** | Per-source-type half-life decay; independence by origin clustering (author/org/near-duplicate text/canonical URL); per-source reliability prior updated by verifier and human corrections; tiers assigned by rule. (`03` §3) |
| 10 | **Measuring genuine novelty?** | Embedding distance from accumulated baseline (B0) outputs, product directories, and prior AOE output, plus a founder "would I have thought of this?" rating. Only meaningful together with evidence strength. (`07` §4) |
| 11 | **Representing financial assumptions and uncertainty?** | (low, base, high) per factor with evidence tier and citations; T6–T7-only factors forced to wide priors; Monte Carlo → P10/P50/P90 MRR and P(≥ $1k), P(≥ $5k); one-at-a-time sensitivity for the weakest link. (`03` §2.2) |
| 12 | **Learning from rejected and validated opportunities?** | Append-only decisions with reason codes and factor snapshots; retrospectives on experiment outcomes; reason-code mining; source-yield bandit; retrieve similar past decisions into prompts (case-based); machine-checkable wake conditions on parked ideas. No fine-tuning. (`07` §8, `05` §2) |
| 13 | **Broad discovery vs. vertical specialisation?** | Explore (weeks 1–4) to pick 2–3 verticals, then ~80/20 exploit/explore. Distribution knowledge, buyer lists, and trust compound inside a vertical and don't transfer. (`02` §7) |
| 14 | **Sandboxing autonomous experimentation?** | Desk research: read-only tools, no credentials. External experiments: only via the approval queue and a deterministic executor. Code: rootless containers, no secrets, egress allowlist, resource/time limits; stronger isolation (gVisor/Firecracker) for untrusted code. (`04` §7–8) |
| 15 | **When to move from research to customer validation?** | When desk research is *saturated* (top-VoE question needs a human/market answer), gates pass, a ≥ 50-org prospect list and channel exist, and thresholds are pre-registered. Cheap 5-conversation probes are allowed as early as *Qualified*. Usually within 1–2 weeks of a dossier. (`05` §4) |
| 16 | **Build locally vs. delegate?** | **Build** (this is the IP): evidence model, extraction + verification, normalised clustering, question-driven scheduler, assessor math, feedback/calibration loop. **Delegate**: search APIs, embeddings, LLMs, queue primitives (Postgres), exploration UI (Datasette/Metabase), notifications, email sending, payments links, hosting. |
| 17 | **Detecting diminishing returns?** | Per opportunity: two consecutive research tasks with no material factor/gate change → saturated. Per source: N runs with no new verified observations → back-off. Per lens: yield of founder-advanced opportunities per $ → bandit reallocation. (`04` §5, `07` §8) |
| 18 | **Simplest path from interesting opportunity to first paying customer?** | Pick a segment where the founder has some access → build a 50-org prospect list → 5–10 problem interviews → offer a **concierge/service-first** version at a real price → deliver manually with internal AI tooling → productise what repeats. Or, where a marketplace exists, ship a narrow plugin for a documented gap. (`03` §8, `05` §5) |
