# Founder self-interview (Phase 0, discovery lens 9)

About 30 minutes. Short, specific answers beat polished ones. Each answer becomes evidence: a direct statement from the founder (tier T2). Name tools, numbers and frequencies wherever you can.

## 1. Tools you built because nothing fit
For each: what it did, who used it, how often, and what you looked at buying first (and why you didn't).

-

## 2. Recurring toil you've seen in more than one organisation
SRE, platform, Kubernetes, Temporal or LLM-infra work that is manual, repetitive, or done with spreadsheets/scripts. Include how often it happens and roughly how many hours it takes.

-

## 3. Vendor products teams overpay for or complain about
Product, what it costs, what people actually use it for, and the complaint.

-

## 4. Work people pay contractors or agencies for in your world
What the deliverable is, roughly what it costs, and who signs off on it.

-

## 5. Communities where you already have credibility
Talks, open-source projects, forums, Slack/Discord groups, ex-colleague networks. Rough size and how you'd reach them.
